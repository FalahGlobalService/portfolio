Quality is the whole product

Bad labels are expensive twice — once to produce, again to find in a trained model. Here is how we keep them out.

Clear guidelines

Written instructions and worked edge cases before the first label, so annotators make the same call on ambiguous data.

Multi-level QA

Several review stages, with reviewers who did not produce the original labels.

Quality metrics

Continuous monitoring on accuracy and completeness KPIs, reported back to you.

Feedback loop

Your corrections go back into the guidelines, so the same error does not appear in the next batch.
